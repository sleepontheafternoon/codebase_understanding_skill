## Priority guide

- **High:** blocks the current flow or could invalidate the architecture model.
- **Medium:** materially improves understanding but does not block the current thread.
- **Low:** local implementation detail or possible future curiosity.

## Closing a question

When resolving a question, record:
1. The conclusion.
2. Supporting source/test/runtime evidence.
3. Whether architecture or flow notes need correction.
4. Remaining limits and edge cases.

Do not delete uncertainty simply because an agent has proposed an explanation.
